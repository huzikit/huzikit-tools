01

Introduction

Welcome to Huzikit (accessible at https://huzikit.com). This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information that may be collected, received, or processed when you visit our website, use our calculation engines, or utilize our developer and content utilities.

Huzikit operates as a web-based productivity hub. Our principal design philosophy is to deliver lightweight, fast, and practical utilities without creating unnecessary barriers to entry. Because different tools throughout the website perform distinct tasks—ranging from mathematical calculations and regex evaluations to image transformations—their technical behaviors vary. This policy clearly describes how information is treated across these scenarios.

By using Huzikit, you acknowledge the terms outlined in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with any practice described here, you may discontinue use of the website or adjust your browser settings accordingly.

02

Information We May Collect

We distinguish strictly between information voluntarily provided by visitors, technical data generated automatically by web browsers, and data entered into individual tools.

Depending on how you interact with Huzikit, information may fall into the following categories:

  • Direct Correspondence: Information you voluntarily share when submitting feedback or support requests via our contact form.
  • Technical and Network Data: Routine data transmitted by your browser during web requests (such as user-agent headers, IP addresses, and referral URLs) processed by hosting or security infrastructure.
  • Tool Inputs: Content, numbers, strings, or files that you input into a tool interface to achieve a specific calculation or conversion result.

We do not harvest or aggregate personal user profiles for marketing, nor do we construct behavioral databases across our users.

03

Information You Provide Directly

Huzikit does not implement a mandatory user account system, registration portal, or paid subscription service for public tools. You are not required to provide your legal name, physical address, phone number, or billing information to access calculators or utilities.

If you choose to communicate with us directly through our official contact page, you may provide your email address, your name or pseudonym, and the content of your message. We use this information solely to review your inquiry, investigate reported technical issues, or respond to your suggestions.

No User Profiles Huzikit does not create private dashboards, store credit card details, or require account logins. You can use our utilities immediately upon opening a page.
04

Information Processed by Huzikit Tools

Because Huzikit hosts a variety of interactive tools, understanding where and how your data is computed is essential. We design our tools to operate as close to the user as possible.

Text & Formatting Tools

Utilities such as Word Counter, Case Converter, Line Removers, and Notepad run via JavaScript inside your browser. The text you input is evaluated locally in temporary memory.

Calculators

Math engines such as Age, BMI, Percentage, and Tip Calculators perform immediate mathematical formulas in client script. Numeric inputs are not stored in database tables.

Developer Utilities

JSON Formatter, Base64 Converter, URL Encoder, and Regex Tester execute through browser runtime logic. Code or string payloads are not retained on our backend.

Image & File Processing

Tools like the Color Picker and QR Generator generate outputs locally. For file operations requiring external libraries, data is handled strictly during execution without persistent storage.

Important Privacy Practice While we do not retain tool inputs, we strongly advise users never to submit sensitive confidential documents, unencrypted private keys, financial credentials, or personally identifiable government records into any public web utility.
05

Browser, Device, and Technical Information

Like nearly all modern web platforms, hosting providers, content delivery networks (CDNs), and network firewalls automatically process certain technical request headers to deliver web pages to your device.

Depending on your browser settings and active network infrastructure, this data may include:

  • Internet Protocol (IP) address (used for network routing and denial-of-service mitigation).
  • Browser vendor, version, and language preferences.
  • Operating system and device screen dimensions.
  • Date and timestamp of HTTP web requests.
  • Referring website addresses and requested URL paths.

This technical telemetry is managed by infrastructure and security providers for stability, load balancing, cyber-attack detection, and firewall enforcement. Huzikit does not use this data to personally identify individual human visitors.

06

Cookies and Similar Technologies

Cookies are small text files stored on your computer or mobile device by websites that you visit. They are widely used to make websites function properly, retain session preferences, or provide usage reports to website owners.

We classify cookies and local browser storage into the following categories:

  • Essential / Technical Storage: Temporary memory or local storage items necessary for page rendering, preventing automated spam, or remembering basic interface states.
  • Preference Storage: Client-side storage (such as localStorage) used strictly by specific tools—for instance, allowing an online scratchpad to keep your draft accessible in your current browser until you clear it.
  • Third-Party Cookies: Cookies set by external domains (such as advertising networks or analytics services) if and when those services are integrated into the website.

You have total authority over cookies. You can instruct your web browser to refuse all cookies, alert you when a cookie is being issued, or wipe all stored cookies and site data at any time via your browser's security preferences.

07

Advertising and Google AdSense

Huzikit may display third-party advertisements, such as those provided through Google AdSense or authorized ad exchanges, to support server costs and keep our tools freely accessible to the public.

When advertising is active on the website, third-party advertising vendors, including Google, may use cookies, web beacons, or mobile advertising identifiers to serve ads based on prior visits to this website or other sites on the internet.

Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve targeted or non-personalized advertisements based on your visit to Huzikit and other digital properties across the web. You can learn more about how Google uses information from sites that use its services by reviewing the Google Advertising Technologies & Policies page.

Visitors may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or through industry opt-out portals such as the Digital Advertising Alliance (aboutads.info) or the Network Advertising Initiative (networkadvertising.org).

08

Analytics and Performance Services

To understand how visitors navigate Huzikit, identify broken tool links, and evaluate site performance across different devices, we may use standard website analytics or server telemetry tools.

When analytics services are active, they collect aggregate metrics such as page views, time spent per utility, bounce rates, and geographic distribution at a country or city level. This data is collected in an aggregated format to help us optimize page loading speeds and prioritize new tool development.

We do not connect aggregate analytical traffic with personal identities or user-entered tool calculations.

09

How We Use Information

Information processed by Huzikit is utilized strictly for legitimate operational purposes:

  • Operating the Service: Rendering website pages, loading scripts, executing requested calculations, and providing responsive outputs.
  • Platform Security: Detecting bot attacks, preventing malicious scraping, mitigating denial-of-service threats, and maintaining server integrity.
  • Bug Fixing & Enhancements: Diagnosing script errors, addressing responsive layout issues, and upgrading tool mathematical accuracy.
  • Customer Support: Replying to direct questions, bug reports, and user feedback sent via our official contact channels.
  • Legal & Compliance Obligations: Meeting applicable regulatory requirements or addressing lawful requests where legally necessary.

We do not use your inputs to train public generative models without consent, nor do we repurpose tool inputs for promotional campaigns.

10

How We Share Information

Huzikit does not sell, rent, or lease your personal information to third parties. We do not participate in cross-context consumer data brokering.

Information may only be shared under the following limited and necessary circumstances:

  • Service Providers & Infrastructure: Trusted cloud hosting providers, network CDNs, and security firewalls that handle data transmission under strict confidentiality requirements.
  • Advertising & Measurement Partners: When third-party ad networks are active, they receive technical request signals directly from your browser to serve advertising units.
  • Compliance with Law: When required by enforceable governmental requests, court orders, or applicable legal statutes.
  • Protection of Rights: When necessary to protect the security, safety, and property of Huzikit, our users, or the general public.
11

Third-Party Services

Huzikit relies on standard modern web services to deliver our content efficiently. These external partners may maintain independent privacy practices:

  • Cloud Infrastructure & Hosting: Servers, SSL certificate authorities, and edge networks that deliver web pages.
  • Web Font Providers: Reputable font delivery networks (such as Google Fonts) that supply the typography used throughout our interface.
  • Advertising Providers: Ad delivery networks such as Google AdSense when advertising spaces are active.

Each third-party service provider operates under its own legal terms and privacy policies. We encourage visitors to review the privacy statements of any third-party services they interact with across the internet.

12

Data Retention

Our data retention practices reflect our minimal data collection philosophy:

  • In-Browser Tool Inputs: Calculations and text entered into client-side utilities exist in your device's memory for the duration of your browser session, or in localStorage until you close the tab or clear your browser data.
  • Support Communications: Inquiries submitted through our contact form are retained only for as long as necessary to address your request, resolve technical issues, or maintain a record of resolved support inquiries.
  • Server & Security Logs: Infrastructure logs maintained by hosting networks are routinely rotated and purged in accordance with standard data retention schedules.

We do not retain long-term archives of your calculations, conversions, or generated passwords.

13

Data Security

We employ industry-standard technical measures designed to protect information transmitted across our web properties. Huzikit enforces HTTPS encryption across all pages, ensuring that communication between your browser and our servers is secured using modern TLS protocols.

However, no method of transmission over the internet or method of electronic storage can be guaranteed 100% impenetrable. While we strive to adopt reasonable safeguards to protect our platform, we cannot guarantee absolute security against all potential cyber threats. Users are encouraged to exercise personal caution when handling sensitive files or credentials on any digital platform.

14

User Choices and Privacy Controls

You retain full control over your interaction with Huzikit. Practical ways you can exercise your privacy preferences include:

  • Browser Settings: You can configure your browser to block cookies, reject third-party tracking, or alert you when cookies are placed.
  • Clearing Local Data: You can clear your browser's cache, cookies, and local storage (localStorage/sessionStorage) at any time to remove saved tool scratchpads or history.
  • Private Browsing: Using incognito or private browsing modes ensures that session cookies, tool inputs, and history are discarded upon closing the window.
  • Advertising Opt-Outs: You can use Google Ad Settings or industry opt-out tools to disable personalized advertising.
  • Content Discretion: You may choose not to provide optional information through contact forms and avoid submitting sensitive information into public web tools.
15

Children's Privacy

Huzikit is designed as a general-audience educational and technical utility website. Our services are not directed toward children under the age of 13 (or under the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction).

We do not knowingly collect, solicit, or maintain personally identifiable information from children. If you believe that a child has provided personal information to Huzikit through a contact submission, please reach out to us via our contact page so that we can promptly remove the communication.

International Users & Regional Privacy Rights

Huzikit is accessible globally. Depending on your geographic location and local data protection regulations (such as the European General Data Protection Regulation, the UK Data Protection Act, or the California Consumer Privacy Act), you may possess specific rights regarding personal data.

Where applicable by law, these rights may include:

  • The right to request access to information we hold about you.
  • The right to request correction or updating of inaccurate personal data.
  • The right to request deletion of personal information held in our records.
  • The right to object to or restrict certain processing activities.

Because Huzikit does not maintain registered user accounts or store tool calculation logs, in most cases we possess no personal identifiable records connected to individual visitors. If you wish to make an inquiry regarding your rights, you may submit a request through our official contact page.
