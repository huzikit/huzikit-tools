How the Calorie Calculator Works

Understanding your daily energy requirements is the foundational step toward achieving your fitness, weight management, or nutritional goals. This calculator utilizes scientifically validated formulas to estimate how much energy your body consumes on a daily basis.

What is BMR (Basal Metabolic Rate)?

Your Basal Metabolic Rate (BMR) represents the total number of calories your body burns while performing basic, life-sustaining functions at complete rest—such as breathing, circulation, cell production, and neurological processing. BMR accounts for roughly 60% to 75% of the total energy your body burns every day.

What is TDEE (Total Daily Energy Expenditure)?

Total Daily Energy Expenditure (TDEE) is an estimation of how many calories you burn per day when factoring in your physical activity level and daily exercise. We determine this by multiplying your calculated BMR by an activity multiplier ranging from sedentary (1.20) to extra active (1.90).
