Essential Percentage Formulas

Master the core mathematical formulas used behind our calculator.

Percentage of a Number

$$\frac{X}{100} \times Y$$

Finds a specific percentage part of any given total number.

Percentage of Total

$$\frac{X}{Y} \times 100$$

Calculates what percentage one number is of another total.

Percentage Increase

$$\frac{\text{New} - \text{Original}}{\text{Original}} \times 100$$

Measures growth rate or price markup between two values.

Percentage Decrease

$$\frac{\text{Original} - \text{New}}{\text{Original}} \times 100$$

Calculates discount rate or markdown percentage.

Percentage Difference

$$\frac{|A - B|}{\frac{A + B}{2}} \times 100$$

Compares two numbers relative to their average value.

Find Original Value

$$\frac{\text{Final}}{1 \pm \frac{\text{Percentage}}{100}}$$

Reverses an increase or decrease to find the initial starting value.

Comprehensive Guide to Percentages

What Is a Percentage?

The term percentage originates from the Latin phrase per centum, meaning "by the hundred." A percentage represents a dimensionless ratio or fraction out of 100. It provides a standard universal scale to compare proportions without needing common denominators.

How Does a Percentage Calculator Work?

Our online calculator applies precise mathematical algorithms to convert inputs into ratios. Whether you need to determine tax amounts, academic grades, financial returns, or statistical variances, the tool automates complex fraction conversions instantly.

Common Percentage Mistakes to Avoid

  • Confusing Percentage Points with Percent: Going from 10% to 15% is a 5 percentage point increase, but a 50% relative increase.
  • Incorrect Base Values: Always ensure the denominator (original value) corresponds to the correct starting quantity in percentage change calculations.
