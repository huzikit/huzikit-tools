What is the Case Converter?

The HuziKit Case Converter is a free online tool that lets you change the capitalization of any text in a single click. Whether you are formatting a headline, cleaning up a pasted paragraph, or preparing copy for a style guide, the tool removes the tedious work of retyping text by hand.

Features

  • Six case styles: uppercase, lowercase, title case, sentence case, capitalized case and alternating case
  • Real-time character, word and line counters
  • Estimated reading time as you type
  • One-click copy to clipboard
  • Works entirely in your browser — nothing is uploaded
  • Fully responsive on phone, tablet and desktop

Benefits

Consistent capitalization improves readability and looks more professional in emails, articles, resumes and code comments. Instead of manually retyping text, the Case Converter lets you fix capitalization instantly, saving time and reducing errors.

How to Use the Case Converter

  1. Paste or type your text into the input box.
  2. Choose a case style from the toolbar above the box.
  3. Review the converted text and check the live word and character counts.
  4. Click "Copy Text" to copy the result to your clipboard.

Examples

Typing "the quick brown fox" and selecting Title Case produces "The Quick Brown Fox". Selecting Sentence case on the same text produces "The quick brown fox." capitalized only at the start.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the HuziKit Case Converter free to use?

Yes. The Case Converter is completely free, with no sign-up and no usage limits.

Does my text get uploaded to a server?

No. All conversion happens locally in your browser, so your text never leaves your device.

What case styles can I convert to?

You can convert text to uppercase, lowercase, title case, sentence case, capitalized case and alternating case.

Can I convert large blocks of text?

Yes. The tool handles long documents and updates word and character counts in real time.

Does the tool work on mobile devices?

Yes. The Case Converter is fully responsive and works on phones, tablets and desktops.

Can I undo a conversion?

Yes. Use the Clear button to reset the input, or simply retype and reapply a different case style at any time.
