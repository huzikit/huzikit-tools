What is a Character Counter?

A character counter is an online utility designed to count every single letter, number, punctuation mark, and whitespace in a given block of text. Whether you are drafting a tweet, crafting meta descriptions for search engine optimization, or formatting an academic essay with strict character limits, our tool delivers instantaneous and pinpoint accuracy.

What Does HuziKit Character Counter Measure?

Our advanced Unicode-aware processing engine calculates comprehensive metrics simultaneously:

  • Characters: Total number of symbols including letters, digits, spaces, and punctuation.
  • Characters Without Spaces: The exact character count excluding all whitespace characters.
  • Words: Total word count separated by spaces or punctuation boundaries.
  • Sentences & Paragraphs: Structural counts based on terminal punctuation (. ! ?) and line breaks.
  • Reading & Speaking Times: Estimated durations based on average human reading speeds (200 words per minute) and speech rates (130 words per minute).
