What is Lorem Ipsum?

Lorem Ipsum is scrambled Latin text derived from a first-century work by Cicero, first used as filler copy by a printer in the 1500s. It has survived five centuries as the default placeholder text for the design industry because its Latin-like rhythm reads like real prose without carrying any actual meaning, which keeps a reader's attention on layout rather than content.

Why Designers Use Lorem Ipsum

When a layout is still being built, real content is often unavailable — copy is still being written, translated, or approved. Lorem Ipsum fills that gap with text that has a natural distribution of short and long words, so headlines, paragraphs and buttons look and behave the way they will once real copy is dropped in. It also avoids the distraction of meaningful words, which can pull a reviewer's attention away from spacing, alignment and typography.
