System Architecture

How Our Contact System Works

We believe in complete transparency about how our contact form communicates with your browser and email client.

Question 1

How can I contact Huzikit?

You can contact Huzikit either by completing the form on this page and clicking "Send Message", or by emailing us directly from your own email account at support@huzikit.com.

Question 2

How does the Huzikit contact form work?

When you fill out your Name, Email, Topic, and Message, our client-side JavaScript formats your message into a structured plain text template, URL-encodes the content safely, and prepares a Gmail Web Compose link.

https://mail.google.com/mail/?view=cm&fs=1&to=support%40huzikit.com&su=...&body=...
Question 3

Does Huzikit send the email itself?

No. Huzikit does NOT send the email on its own servers, nor do we use third-party form-forwarding services. The email is sent by you through Gmail when you review and press Gmail's own "Send" button.

Question 4

Does the contact form open Gmail?

Yes. Clicking "Send Message" immediately opens a new browser tab with Gmail's Compose screen pre-populated with recipient support@huzikit.com, your topic as the subject line, and your message in the body. If your browser blocks popups, a direct clickable fallback link is displayed right away.
